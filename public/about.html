GAME OVERVIEW

ORBIS — Flight & Shipping Simulator

ORBIS is a free, browser-based flight and shipping simulator built around a world map. Search airports and seaports, create custom routes, dispatch aircraft and ships, and develop a connected transport network.

Route planning, airline management and shipping simulation share the same logistics system. Fuel, weather snapshots, ocean conditions and delayed connections affect your operations, so each decision can influence later journeys. Progress is saved in your browser and can be exported for transfer.

存档与模拟规则

游戏在访问者自己的浏览器中计算和绘制。进度保存在当前浏览器；切换设备或网址前可以导出 JSON，再在另一处导入。

这是基于真实地理设施的模拟运输游戏。航班和航次由玩家创建，运输结果由游戏规则计算；世界环境采用周期更新的数据快照。
